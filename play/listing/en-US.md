# RufiTrack — English (en-US) Play listing draft

App name: RufiTrack
Category: Finance
Pricing: Free
Short description: Track expenses, budgets and bank transactions in MVR.

Full description:
RufiTrack is a personal money tracker designed for spending in Maldivian rufiyaa (MVR).

Bring your transactions together by importing bank statements, adding entries manually or optionally recognizing supported Maldives Islamic Bank (MIB) and Bank of Maldives (BML) transaction messages.

See spending by category and merchant, set budgets and savings goals, review possible duplicates, reconcile statement activity and explore trends. Account balance projections are estimates derived from your records, not live bank balances.

Your transaction ledger is stored on your device. You decide whether to grant SMS and notification access, and can manage records through the app's import, review, backup and deletion tools.

RufiTrack is independent of MIB and BML and is not a banking service. Always verify account balances and transactions with your bank.
