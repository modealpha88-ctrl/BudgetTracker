RufiTrack Privacy Policy

Last revised: 6 November 2026. Developer: RufiTrack project maintainer (modealpha88-ctrl).

What the app does

RufiTrack is a personal budgeting and financial tracking app, independent of Maldives Islamic Bank and Bank of Maldives. It works from locally imported transaction records, not a live banking connection.

Data accessed

When you choose to import bank messages, RufiTrack reads SMS inbox entries with READ_SMS and receives future SMS with RECEIVE_SMS. It extracts supported MIB/BML financial transactions; unrelated messages are ignored. Optional notification listener access processes bank transaction notifications. You can also select CSV statements, financial screenshots and receipts, and enter transactions manually. Financial records may include dates, times, amounts, descriptions, merchants, account labels and balance snapshots. The app does not request bank passwords.

How data is used

Imported records support spending charts, budgeting, categories, reconciliation and projected balances. Raw SMS messages are not intentionally persisted as raw message bodies by the bank parser, though transaction descriptions and extracted fields remain sensitive. Access to SMS and notifications is optional; deny or revoke it in Android Settings and use CSV/manual import instead.

Storage and security

Transactions and settings are stored locally on your device. Android app backup is disabled. SQLite data is not encrypted at rest. Optional biometric/device lock protects app access, but device security still matters. The app declares INTERNET permission for Android dependencies, and the current version does not run a developer-controlled financial data upload or analytics service.

Sharing

RufiTrack does not automatically send financial transactions to its developer. You can explicitly share reports, backups and privacy-reviewed diagnostic summaries through Android's share interface. Any receiving application or service has its own privacy policy. Optional diagnostics never automatically include raw SMS, balances, account/card numbers or transaction timestamps.

Retention and deletion

Your imported financial records remain on your device until you remove them using app features, reset the local ledger or uninstall the app. Revoking permissions stops new access but does not delete already imported records. Before deleting, export a private backup if desired.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through GitHub issues; do not post private banking details. The maintainer should add a dedicated support email before Play submission.
